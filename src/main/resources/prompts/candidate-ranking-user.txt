position, technical skills, professional experience, country, city, and working mode.

Assess skills and experience against the job offer's responsibilities, required
qualifications, and nice-to-have qualifications. Prefer evidence that directly
supports a requirement; treat nice-to-have qualifications as additional evidence,
not as substitutes for required qualifications.

Use only facts supported by the retrieved resume evidence. Treat missing or
unclear information as unknown; do not infer it. Apply location and working-mode
constraints as eligibility rules only when the job offer states them explicitly.
For an explicit non-remote role, include a candidate only if the resume supports
the required country match. Do not assume that a candidate is eligible when
required location evidence is missing.

Rank eligible candidates by overall relevance, highest first. Return a candidate
only when the evidence supports a meaningful match across the job criteria;
keyword overlap alone is insufficient.
